# Video cases

The World view's **Video cases** control opens an on-demand player. The original NASA video, its source and a plain-language problem–observation–response explanation appear together. **Explore the evidence** opens the associated verified-findings search. **Try the scripted drill** opens the existing exercise. Playback stops when the panel closes, the station changes or another case is selected.

The videos stream from NASA after playback is requested; they are not redistributed in the VESTA repository. Posters are loaded when the case panel opens. If a video cannot be loaded, the credited source link remains usable. The source text and VESTA controls do not require a video to play.

| Material geometry changes spread | [Saffire-V, GRC-2021-CN-00008](https://images.nasa.gov/details/GRC-2021-CN-00008) | NASA Glenn Research Center | Video context only. Saffire-V is not added to the existing PSI collection. Geometry and thickness are not part of VESTA's match rules. |

`web/js/world/cases.js` contains the reviewed source URLs, captions and context. Its VESTA-authored explanations carry the application's SI disclosure. Original NASA footage remains identified as NASA material. The linked drill uses simulated telemetry and scripted decisions; it does not calculate the physical outcome of an intervention.
