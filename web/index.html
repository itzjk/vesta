One result the rows do support: in NASA’s FLEX droplet tests (liquid fuel in microgravity, not cabin materials) the flame went out on its own in 103/115 tests at 18% oxygen or less, 63/91 at 18–22%, 20/59 at 22–30% and 0/9 above 30%. A logistic model on oxygen and fuel, checked on 66 held-out test days, gets 179/224 right vs 150/224 for the majority baseline.
