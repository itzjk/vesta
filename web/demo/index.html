VESTA: 29-second demo

This is a recording of the live VESTA app, operated with its real controls. No image or clip in it was generated. The app's visuals are drawn by code written with SI assistance from Claude (Anthropic) and Codex (OpenAI), and carry the mark “SI-assisted visuals”. The captions were added in the edit. The music is original: it was synthesized by code written with SI assistance, with no samples and no third-party recording. There is no narration.

The droplet flame at 12 seconds is original NASA footage of the Flame Extinguishment Experiment (FLEX) on the International Space Station, played inside the app. Credit: Science@NASA and NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center, Scientific Visualization Studio, 11318. It shows a fuel-droplet test in microgravity; it is not evidence for a lunar cabin, and NASA does not endorse VESTA.

The candle flame in the first seconds is an illustration of low-momentum flames, not a simulation. The telemetry and decisions of the drill are scripted, not a physics model. VESTA is a prototype: it is not flight software and it has not been validated by NASA.
