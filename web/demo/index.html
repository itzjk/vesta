VESTA: 29-second demo

This explainer combines recordings of the live VESTA app, operated with its real controls, with animated titles, counters and charts drawn by code. No image or clip in it was produced by an image or video generator. The app's visuals and the animated graphics were made with SI assistance from Claude (Anthropic) and Codex (OpenAI) and carry the mark “SI-assisted visuals”. The narration is a team member's own recorded voice; no synthetic or cloned voice is used, and there is no music.

Every number on screen comes from the app's data: 1,989 NASA source-table rows, 0 of 45 questions with a nearby row for a lunar cabin, 274 recorded FLEX droplet tests by oxygen band, and limiting-oxygen values for Mylar G printed in a NASA report (Olson and Ferkul, 2012). Rows are table records, not independent experiments.

The humanoid is a simulation: no hardware, sensors or autonomy. VESTA shows evidence coverage, not a safety rating, and is not endorsed by NASA.
