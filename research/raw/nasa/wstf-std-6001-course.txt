# Flight Acceptance Standard Testing (NASA-STD-6001) Familiarization Course

Heather F. Riley

Nov 06, 2019

Article

Flammability engineers evaluating posttest flammability samples

NASA-WSTF

White Sands Test Facility offers a familiarization course to help our customers navigate the requirements of flammability, offgas toxicity, volatile condensable materials, and fluid compatibility testing contained in the NASA Technical Standards

- “Flammability, Offgassing, and Compatibility Requirements and Test Procedures” NASA-STD-6001,

- “Standard Materials and Processes Requirements for Spacecraft“, NASA-STD-6016.

Overview

Our experts will assist your NASA organization and partners with understanding the requirements, testing capabilities, customization options, and test request process including the

- Purpose and application for each test,

- Variations of each test and when they are warranted,

- Testing exemptions,

- Custom testing to meet your specific needs,

- Historical data (data resources/application limitations),

- Test request process, and

- Materials and processes role/interface.

Target audience

Any NASA organizations and their partners who design and develop manned spacecraft or hardware
