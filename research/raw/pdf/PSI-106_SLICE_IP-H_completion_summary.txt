                Structure & Liftoff In Combustion Experiment (SLICE)
                Research Review Board – Completed Milestone
Task Objective:                                                                                                                Project: ISS Research
     Improve our understanding of the physical and chemical processes controlling non-premixed flame structure and stabilization.
     Provide for rigorous testing of numerical models including thermal radiation, soot formation, and detailed chemical kinetics.
     Refine the upcoming ACME experiment Coflow Laminar Diffusion Flame (CLD Flame) to minimize risk and maximize science.
     Extend the Smoke Point In Coflow Experiment (SPICE) with additional conditions (e.g., fuel) and data.
