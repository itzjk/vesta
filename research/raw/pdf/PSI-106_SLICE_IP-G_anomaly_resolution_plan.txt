       SLICE FORWARD PLAN: Jan. 26
   •     Problem
           –    The video overlay of data (e.g., flow conditions) is not working properly, where the titler is
                now in its HEX trouble-shooting mode putting out scrolling hexadecimal characters.
           –    While tests could still be conducted, this would be a MAJOR science loss and would include
                reliance on a partial calibration conducted at the end of SPICE ops in 2009 because the fan
                calibration planned for every session can not be carried out without the data
   •     Hardware Recovery
           –    A procedure has been developed and tested on the ground for full hardware recovery, i.e., to
                return the titler to its normal mode with the proper video overlay of the SPICE hardware data.
           –    The one-time procedure requires:
                  • recharging the titler batteries which are now strongly believed to be present
                  • opening the SPICE video unit (2 non-captive fasteners)
                  • scraping conformal coating off of 5 exposed pins on the titler circuit board
                  • making a series of temporary jumper connections between pairs of those pins to change
                     the titler mode from HEX to NOR(mal) while the SPICE hardware is powered, where:
                       – the crew can see titler setup menu via the video overlay
                       – the video unit is at 12 volts, so there is no hazard of electrical shock
                  • turning the hardware off (once) and back on, where SPICE’s onboard computer will then
                     reset all other titler settings
           –    In contrast to our discussion on Tuesday, the procedure does not involve battery removal.
           –    Needed tools are available on orbit (e.g., in the ISS Pin Kit and CRE-1 tool kit)
           –    Procedure development is in progress, where a draft is available
