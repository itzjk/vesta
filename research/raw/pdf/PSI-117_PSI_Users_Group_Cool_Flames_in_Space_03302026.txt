Counterflow Diffusion Flame




      Cool diffusion flame, 1 g (Won et al.)
                                                Excellent configuration for studying flame’s strength under the
                                                 effect of stretch.
                                                Useful platform for validating reaction kinetics and transport
                                                 models.
                                                Idealized condition, it can be treated as a 1D problem:

                                                    Leads to the derivation of an analytical solution
                                                    Makes numerical modeling tractable
Limitations of the conventional numerical approach
      The numerical effort to study counterflow flame configuration has been predominantly centered
       upon OPPDIF module of CHEMKIN chemical kinetic package
      This module implements a similarity transformation approach that reduces the flow field to a
       one-dimensional/ plug flow problem with the following assumptions:

         Radial component of velocity is linear in radius
         Dependent variables become functions of the axial direction only




                    What are the physical conditions that can be
                         represented by this assumption?
