1.0    INTRODUCTION

The purpose of this document is to identify the science requirements for the SPICE Experiment
manifested to fly on the ISS. The science requirements are traced to the Science Requirements
Document.


2.0    VERIFICATION CLOSURE RECORD

Appendix A contains each Science Verification Sheet, which identifies the science requirement,
verification method, and verification closure summary. The verification summary identifies the
SPICE project document that addresses the verification closure. All SPICE project documents
are under configuration control and available upon request. This document will be updated
periodically to reflect the closure status of the verifications.


3.0    CURRENT VERIFICATION STATUS

There are 18 verification requirements. All requirements have been closed. One verification,
Radiometer accuracy is not compliant.

required.

The accuracy is stated to be ±3% of the reading ±1% of the selected range. This
is within the required accuracy.

Inspection of the data acquired and stored in the Experiment Assembly shows
that 4 samples per second are acquired.

VERIFICATION: 9                           ERD PARAGRAPH:

VERIFICATION LEAD(S):

REQUIREMENT

The Co-Flow Fan Voltage shall be measured with an accuracy of 10% of the
reading, at a sampling rate of 4 Hz, and over a range of 0.5 – 12 volts.




IMPLEMENTATION

The Co-Flow Fan Voltage signal will be monitored via a voltage divider
proportional to the voltage in the range of 0.5 to 12 VDC. This signal will be
monitored by the A/D Converter on the Tattletale board.



VERIFICATION                              VERIFICATION METHOD: TEST


Perform a functional test per SPICE-TST-006. The functional test will verify
that when the Fan Speed potentiometer is set at its lowest and highest setting
that the measurement is not out of range and that the change is as expected.




VERIFICATION STATUS: CLOSED - COMPLIANT

The fan voltage minimum is 5.5 volts due to the safety requirement that the fan
provide airflow at all times even when the Fan Flow Potentiometer is set to zero.
 The measurement displayed on the video monitor and in the data ranges from
55 to 139. This represents 5.5 volts – 13.9 volts going to the fan.

Inspection of the data acquired and stored in the Experiment Assembly shows
that 4 samples per second are acquired.

VERIFICATION: 10                    ERD PARAGRAPH:

VERIFICATION LEAD(S):

REQUIREMENT

The Co-Flow Velocity shall be measured with an accuracy of 10% of the
reading, at a sampling rate of 4 Hz, and over a range of 5 – 50 cm/s.




IMPLEMENTATION

The Oxidizer Velocity and Co-Flow Velocity are the same measurement and
they will be measured by an Air Velocity Transducer made by TSI with Model
number 8475. The output is routed to an amplifier for scaling with the output
monitored by the A/D Converter on the Tattletale board.


VERIFICATION                               VERIFICATION METHOD: INS


Verify that the Velocity Probe is capable of supporting the required range and
accuracy.

If time permits the probe will be calibrated.



VERIFICATION STATUS: CLOSED - COMPLIANT

The specification sheet for the Velocity Probe states that the velocity range is
from 0.05 to 2.54 m/s (5 -254 cm/s) which is within the range of 5 – 50 cm/s
required.
The fact that the fan is always on limits the lower range that the Velocity Probe
reaches. The measurement displayed on the video monitor and in the data
ranges from 15 to 53 CFM for minimum fan velocity to maximum fan velocity.
The accuracy is stated to be ±3% of the reading ±1% of the selected range. This
is within the required accuracy.
Inspection of the data acquired and stored in the Experiment Assembly shows
that 4 samples per second are acquired.

VERIFICATION: 11                          ERD PARAGRAPH:

VERIFICATION LEAD(S):

REQUIREMENT

The Flame Radiation shall be measured with an accuracy of 10% of the reading,
at a sampling rate of 4 Hz, and at a wavelength from visible to near infra-red.




IMPLEMENTATION

The Flame Radiation measurement will be made by a Thermopile Detector
made by ORIEL with Model number 71768. The output is routed to an
amplifier for scaling and amplification with the output monitored by the A/D
Converter on the Tattletale board.


VERIFICATION                              VERIFICATION METHOD: INS


Verify that the Radiometer is capable of measuring from visible to near infra-
red.




VERIFICATION STATUS: CLOSED – NOT COMPLIANT

The specification sheet for the Thermopile Detector states that the CAF2 coated
version present in the SPICE system has a spectral range of 0.13 to 11 µm. This
range encompasses the visible and near infrared as required.

The Radiometer is highly susceptible to EMI. Accurate readings can only be
done by comparing the value prior to flame initiation and during flame activity.

Inspection of the data acquired and stored in the Experiment Assembly shows
that 4 samples per second are acquired.
